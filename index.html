About Syria

Syria is one of the oldest inhabited regions in the world, home to remarkable historical landmarks, vibrant cities, breathtaking mountains, and a beautiful Mediterranean coast. With a history spanning thousands of years, Syria has been a crossroads of civilizations and cultures.

From the lively streets of Damascus to the majestic Citadel of Aleppo and the ancient ruins of Palmyra, every destination tells a unique story that has shaped human history.

Popular Syrian Cities

Damascus

One of the world's oldest continuously inhabited capitals, famous for its ancient souks, Umayyad Mosque, and rich cultural heritage.

Aleppo

Home to the iconic Citadel of Aleppo and one of the oldest cities in history, known for its architecture, markets, and traditions.

Top Attractions

Umayyad Mosque

One of the oldest and most significant mosques in the world, located in the heart of Damascus.

Citadel of Aleppo

An impressive medieval fortress overlooking the historic city of Aleppo.

Krak des Chevaliers

One of the world's finest preserved medieval castles and a UNESCO World Heritage Site.

Palmyra

The legendary ancient oasis city famous for its magnificent Roman ruins.

Bosra Roman Theatre

A remarkably preserved Roman theatre built from black basalt stone.

Apamea

An ancient city renowned for its spectacular Roman colonnaded avenue stretching nearly two kilometers.
